Web Application

VegeHut.lk

VegeHut is an innovative web platform that bridges the gap between Sri Lankan farmers and wholesale vegetable buyers. By providing a direct digital marketplace, it streamlines the procurement process, eliminates middlemen, and ensures fair trade. The platform empowers farmers with digital access to a wider market and gives buyers real-time visibility into available produce, creating a more efficient and equitable agricultural supply chain.
